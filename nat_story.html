Notes

I didn’t start this because I thought I could fix anything.

I started because doing nothing felt worse.


People keep asking what difference it makes.

Like change is supposed to be visible right away.

Like it should feel immediate.


But nothing on this scale works like that.

The planet doesn’t move at our pace.

It never has.


Ice melts slowly.

Oceans rise quietly.

Forests disappear one section at a time.


By the time you can feel it, it’s already been happening for years.


We are living inside changes we can’t fully perceive.


That’s what makes it so easy to ignore.

Not because it isn’t real.

Because it isn’t immediate.


I know I won’t see the outcome of anything I do.

That’s not how this works.


The Anthropocene means we are part of something larger than ourselves.

Not just living in it.

Changing it.


Even if the results don’t happen now,

they still happen.


Maybe nothing I do matters on its own.

But nothing only happens on its own.


Everything adds up.

Even the parts you can’t see yet.



Crutzen (2002); Chakrabarty (2009); Clark (2011); Wajcman (2015)
