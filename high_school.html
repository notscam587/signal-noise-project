Group Project: Our Friend Group

This is our updated project for high school.


Introduction

Our group has known each other for a long time, starting in elementary school.

We decided to continue working together for this project.


About Us

We used to spend most of our time together outside.

Now we mostly stay in contact through our phones.

It makes communication easier and faster.

We don’t really think about what that changes.


Daily Activities

We all have different schedules now because of school and other responsibilities.

Because of this, we usually talk through group chats instead of meeting in person.

Sometimes we still hang out.


Group Members

Conclusion

Even though things have changed, we are still able to stay connected.

Technology has made it easier for us to communicate and stay updated with each other.

We are still close as a group.

Just in different ways now.
